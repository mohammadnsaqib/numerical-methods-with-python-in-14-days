the implicit method evaluates the equation about ten times per step, and the
finite-difference Jacobian costs most of those. At the coarse end it still
wins: 600 calls beat 1500 with a smaller error, because stability does not
hold its step down. For a tight error, explicit Euler at h = 0.0005 wins.
