Heun and the midpoint rule have the same stability polynomial, so the same limit.
RK4 survives a step 1.3926 times longer than Euler's, for four times the work.
A higher order buys accuracy. It does not buy much stability, and Day 14 shows
what to reach for when the step this allows is still far too short.
