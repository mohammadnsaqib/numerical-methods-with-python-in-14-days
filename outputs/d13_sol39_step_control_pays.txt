At tol 1e-10 the adaptive rule beats 3200 fixed steps by six decades on a third
of the evaluations. At tol 1e-6 it misses the pulse entirely and reports no
trouble, and a smaller first step does not save it: the controller is allowed to
grow the step fivefold per success, so by t = 3 it is wide again. An error
estimate is a statement about the step that WAS taken, never about what was
skipped over.
